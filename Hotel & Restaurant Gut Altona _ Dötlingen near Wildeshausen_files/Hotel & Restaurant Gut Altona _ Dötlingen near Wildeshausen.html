Hotel & Restaurant in the Wildeshausen Geest

What began as a forest and mill estate in 1888 has become one of the largest hotels in the Oldenburg district - grown over five generations and still family-owned today.

Centrally located yet peaceful: an ensemble of lakes, the Mühlbach stream, and extensive woodlands, just minutes from the A1 motorway. Gut Altona lies near Wildeshausen in the triangle between Oldenburg, Bremen, and Vechta – about 25 minutes from Oldenburg and just over half an hour from Bremen.

Accommodation is available in five room categories, spread across five buildings on the estate. In the restaurant, head chef Georg Schumacher and his team prepare regional and seasonal dishes, and the evening concludes at the Bar 1888.

The entire estate is used for celebrations and meetings: Eleven rooms with natural light are available for conferences, while the banquet halls, the conservatory, and the festival barn are perfect for parties. Throughout the year, the barn hosts cabbage walks, Oktoberfest celebrations, and Christmas parties.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Gut Altona located?
Gut Altona is located at Wildeshauser Str. 34 in 27801 Dötlingen, in the heart of the Wildeshausen Geest region. You can reach us from Oldenburg and Bremen via the A1 motorway, Wildeshausen-Nord exit, avoiding city traffic: it's about 2 kilometers to Wildeshausen, about 32 minutes to Bremen, and about 25 minutes to Oldenburg.
What does Gut Altona offer?
Gut Altona is a family-run estate hotel with 87 rooms, a restaurant, the Bar 1888, function rooms for celebrations and weddings, and eleven conference rooms. The business has been run by the Ahrmann family since 1888 – now in its fifth generation, with approximately 115 employees.
How do I reserve a table at the restaurant?
The quickest way to book a table is online via this website or by phone at +49 4431 9500. The kitchen is open Monday to Saturday from 5:30 pm to 9:00 pm, and business lunch is served Monday to Friday from 12:00 pm to 1:30 pm. Sunday is our day off – the restaurant and bar close after breakfast.
Are there free parking spaces?
Around the main building, over 150 free parking spaces are available – even during events. Electric cars can be charged at eight charging points in the parking lot opposite. Payment at the charging points is accepted via standard charging cards or credit card.
How far is Gut Altona from Wildeshausen, Oldenburg and Bremen?
The town center of Wildeshausen is about 2 kilometers away, roughly a 4-minute drive; the same distance to Wildeshausen train station. Oldenburg is about 35 kilometers and about 35 minutes away, Bremen about 37 kilometers and about 32 minutes. Bremen Airport is about a 30-minute drive, and Bremen Central Station about 36 minutes. Bus line 270, which runs between Oldenburg Central Station and Wildeshausen, stops right outside the door, and Wildeshausen is on the Oldenburg-Osnabrück railway line with hourly service.
Where does the name Gut Altona come from?
From Low German. In the 16th century, Count Anton I of Oldenburg had a watermill built on the Pennigsbäke stream. Until then, the residents of Wildeshausen had to have their grain milled in Wildeshausen itself and were now expected to use the new Oldenburg mill. From their perspective, it was far too close to the town, "all to nah" in Low German – hence the name Altona. This explanation is also given by the Wildeshauser Geest Nature Park; the name has nothing to do with Altona in Hamburg.
What is there to experience around the estate?
The estate includes 70 hectares of forest, which our guests are welcome to use, and three lakes, two of which are open for fishing with a license, plus a backwater for fishing. Our guests can play tennis free of charge on the courts of the tennis club right next to the house, a one-minute walk away – please reserve in advance at reception. Bicycles are available for rent on-site, and the Wildeshauser Geest Golf Club is about 4 km away, with four other golf courses within a 25 km radius. The Wildeshauser Geest Nature Park begins right outside the door – the Huntepad hiking trail starts at our driveway.
How can I pay?
Cash up to €9,500, with debit card, credit card or American Express, on account and with voucher.
Where can I get a voucher for Gut Altona?
Via the voucher page on this website or directly at our reception on site.
What seasonal dates should I be aware of?
From January to March, we host cabbage-themed parties in the barn; on Easter Sunday, we offer an Easter brunch; and on Christmas Day and Boxing Day, we serve a Christmas menu and buffet. We are closed for our annual holiday on New Year's Eve. At Pentecost, we are usually busy with the Guild Festival ourselves, but we also offer our premises by prior arrangement.
How do I reach the right contact person for my inquiry?
The easiest way is by phone at +49 4431 9500 - our hotline will direct you to the right person as soon as you have described your request, whether it concerns a room, a celebration, a wedding or a conference.
How large is the team at Gut Altona?
With approximately 115 employees, which equates to about 85 full-time positions, Gut Altona is one of the larger employers in the Oldenburg district.
What is Gut Altona doing for sustainability?
Photovoltaic systems with a capacity of 220 kWp, a wood chip heating system that is fueled in part by wood from our own forestry, and a water purification system that filters our tap water and prepares it for conferences and events. In addition, we offer regional Vilsa water, beef from Northern Germany, an electric vehicle charging station with 8 charging points powered mostly by green electricity, many products from local producers – from the baker in Wildeshausen to the asparagus farm in Harpstedt – and 80% reusable cloth napkins.
Are dogs allowed at Gut Altona?
Yes. Dogs are welcome in some rooms upon prior arrangement; we charge €12.50 per room per night. In the restaurant, they are allowed in the fireplace room and the summer garden, as well as in all function rooms and the estate garden. Please keep your dog on a leash throughout the grounds and in the buildings and clean up after them immediately. The Wildeshauser Geest Nature Park begins right at the entrance to the property, with hiking trails starting at the driveway.
